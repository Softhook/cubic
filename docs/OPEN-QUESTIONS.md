# Open Rules Questions

A tabletop group can resolve an ambiguity with a shrug; a rules engine can't. Each
item below needs a ruling before (or while) the engine implements it. The
**Proposed ruling** is a default — change it, then record the decision in
[RULES.md](RULES.md) and mark the item ✅.

Legend: 🔴 blocks the core engine · 🟡 needed for a specific card/feature · 🟢 polish · ✅ settled by a cited source or a dated decision

---

## Core rules

| # | | Question | Proposed ruling |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ✅ | **Conquer sum** — must *all* your ships in orbital positions be counted, or may you pick a subset? | All of your ships in the 4 orbital positions count; enemy and diagonal ships are ignored. *2013 rulebook p.7: "If your ships in orbital positions add up to a higher or lower number, you cannot construct."* |
| 2 | ✅ | **Infamy timing** — immediately on reaching 6 (print edition) or in the end phase (stolksdorf CE)? | Immediately; play continues, and it can win the game mid-turn. *2013 rulebook p.9: "Place the quantum cube immediately - do not wait until the end of your turn."* The CE print edition agrees. Dominance resets to 1 when the cube is placed; with nowhere to place it, dominance stays at 6 and Infamy triggers again on the next gain (*2013 rulebook p.9: "After you have placed your quantum cube, reset your dominance die to 1"*). |
| 3 | ✅ | **Movement** — "up to N": must a ship move at least 1? Does the attacked space count as one of the N steps? | At least 1 step. Entering the enemy's space costs a movement point (*2013 rulebook p.6*). A path is a sequence of orthogonal steps through empty spaces. |
| 4 | ✅ | **Repel** — "back to its last space" means the space it occupied immediately before the attack step? | Yes: "moves back into the square from which it attacked" (*2013 rulebook p.6*; also the [add-on pack](../reference/addon-pack-2014/TRANSCRIPT.md) FAQ: not back to where its move started). |
| 5 | ✅ | **Per-die tracking** — "each die can move once / use one ability, even if it changes type". | Per die id, not per value: "Even if the number of a ship changes, a single ship die can only move/attack once per turn, and can only use one special ability per turn" (*2013 rulebook p.4*). This holds even if the ship is destroyed ([add-on pack](../reference/addon-pack-2014/TRANSCRIPT.md) expert FAQ). |
| 6 | ✅ | **Deployed ship** — can a ship deployed this turn also move this turn? | Yes: "you can deploy a ship and move it in the same turn" (*2013 rulebook p.5*; [add-on pack](../reference/addon-pack-2014/TRANSCRIPT.md) expert FAQ). |
| 7 | ✅ | **Reconfigure history** — "until it shows a value it has not had this turn". Does the value it was rolled to on destruction count? | Decided (2026-10-03, [RULE-SUGGESTIONS](RULE-SUGGESTIONS.md)), no source: keep the engine. History starts at the value at the start of the turn (or when entering the scrapyard). Not settled by BGG. Related designer rulings: a destroyed ship is rolled once and may show its old number; a chosen reroll must give a new number ([BGG 1146181](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1146181)); a die keeps its used move and ability through the scrapyard ([BGG 1070681](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1070681)). |
| 8 | ✅ | **Card limits & empty decks** — what happens when the Skill/Tactic deck or face-up row runs out? | Reshuffle discards into a new deck; if still empty, fewer face-up cards. *2013 rulebook p.9: "If there are no more cards in a deck, shuffle the discards and make a new deck."* |
| 9 | ✅ | **Multiple cards in phase 2** — order of taking (conquer cards vs research card)? | Player chooses order; take one card, refill its slot, then pick the next. A Gambit resolves as soon as it is taken (designer, [BGG 1068669](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1068669)), and Gambits can't be kept for a later turn ([add-on pack](../reference/addon-pack-2014/TRANSCRIPT.md) FAQ), so a Momentum turn is played before the next pick (BGA; forum, [BGG 1260830](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1260830), [3211303](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3211303)). Engine: picks still owed are carried into the Momentum turn's card phase. |
| 10 | ✅ | **Combat roll pipeline** — order of *set* effects (Rational = 3, Plan Ahead = 1, Missile = 1, Brutal) vs *modifiers* (Ferocious −1, Strategic −2). Can a roll go below 1? | Decided (2026-10-03, [RULE-SUGGESTIONS](RULE-SUGGESTIONS.md)): keep this order. If Rational and Plan Ahead both apply, the lower (1) wins. `roll (Brutal: min of 2)` → `set (Rational / Plan Ahead)` → `missile override` → `modifiers`. Rolls may go to 0 or below: "the −1 modifier is to the combat number (not the die faces)" (designer, [BGG 1069897](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1069897); [add-on pack](../reference/addon-pack-2014/TRANSCRIPT.md): "your total combat value can drop below 1 with cards like Ferocious and Strategic"). Modifiers apply to Rational's 3 (forum, no designer post, [BGG 1137988](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1137988), [1165528](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1165528)). The missile and Plan Ahead steps are CE only and have no source. |
| 11 | ✅ | **Planet capacity** — Infamy and Entanglement still require an empty cube location? | Yes. Infamy: "any empty cube location on a planet where you do NOT already have a cube" (*2013 rulebook p.9*). Entanglement: designer errata (BGG thread 1087563) and the [add-on pack](../reference/addon-pack-2014/TRANSCRIPT.md): only when the sole free space on the whole map is on a planet where you already have a cube. Infamy may then place there too ([add-on pack](../reference/addon-pack-2014/TRANSCRIPT.md)). |
| 12 | ✅ | **Quantum Entanglement** — target is `planet + 3 × your cubes there`. With Intelligent (±1) and Pioneering/Tyrannical? | Decided (2026-10-03, [RULE-SUGGESTIONS](RULE-SUGGESTIONS.md)): the order doesn't matter. (planet ± 1) + 3k = (planet + 3k) ± 1, and Pioneering and Tyrannical change the ship side of the sum, not the target. |
| 13 | ✅ | **Expansion with empty reserve** — can you take the card? | No — Expansion is not selectable with an empty reserve, in the Gambit row (Original) or the Expansion pile (CE). *2013 rulebook p.9: "If you already have both of your expansion ships in the game, you cannot use EXPANSION cards."* |
| 14 | ✅ | **Starting planets** — what if a map has more starting planets than players? | Players pick in turn order from any marked starting planet. All cubes are placed first, then each player arranges their ships in player order (*2013 rulebook p.3*). |
| 15 | ✅ | **Void tile research** — capped at 6? Triggers a card? | Decided (2026-10-03, [RULE-SUGGESTIONS](RULE-SUGGESTIONS.md)): Capped at 6; card is taken in phase 2 as normal. |

## Ship abilities

| # | | Question | Proposed ruling |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16 | ✅ | **Battlestation Free Attack** — usable if the die already moved this turn? | Yes. It does not use the die's move. "It is possible to move/attack normally and also use strike for a second attack on the same turn" (*2013 rulebook p.8*). |
| 17 | ✅ | **Flagship carry** — passenger picked from a surrounding space (diagonal OK). Can the passenger have already moved? Does it then still get its own move? | Being carried is not the passenger's move: it may be carried whether or not it has moved, and may still move afterwards if it hasn't. A carried ship may be carried again, and a carried flagship may then transport another ship ("triple-Flagship slingshot", designer, [BGG 1113798](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1113798)). Pickup happens before the move, drop-off after; no pickups en route. The flagship may fly out and back to its own space ("zero move" transport, designer, [BGG 1074052](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1074052)). It must move 1–2 spaces to transport and can't attack while transporting; the passenger is dropped on an empty space and may move/attack afterwards as a separate action ([add-on pack](../reference/addon-pack-2014/TRANSCRIPT.md) FAQ). |
| 18 | ✅ | **Destroyer swap** — any distance? | Any of your ships on the map, any distance. Warp does not count as the destroyer's move (*2013 rulebook p.8*). |
| 19 | ✅ | **Frigate → 3/5** — can it then use the new type's ability (swap, or diagonal move)? | No: "After you modify, you can't use the ship's new ability this turn" (*2013 rulebook p.8*). |
| 20 | ✅ | **Interceptor diagonal** — may it squeeze diagonally between two blocking pieces? | Yes; only the destination square must be empty, including diagonal steps past a planet's corner. It may mix diagonal and orthogonal steps in one move. Forum consensus, no designer post ([BGG 1286057](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1286057), [1558524](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1558524)). |

## Interrupts (critical for online play)

| # | | Question | Proposed ruling |
|---|---|---|---|
| 21 | ✅ | **Missiles "at any time"** — online, we need explicit priority windows. When exactly? | Decided (2026-10-03, [RULE-SUGGESTIONS](RULE-SUGGESTIONS.md)), no source: engine today. No pre-roll window, since Tactics resolve when taken and Dangerous has its own prompt (#22). One window after both combat dice are rolled and before resolution. Every player (in turn order from attacker) may respond; window repeats until all pass. Configurable timer. |
| 22 | ✅ | **Dangerous** — defender decides before the roll, on the opponent's turn. | The defender is asked after the attack is declared, before any dice are rolled. Destroying both ships changes no dominance and triggers no "when you destroy" card (Plundering, Warlike…) for either side (designer, [BGG 1070690](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1070690); [add-on pack](../reference/addon-pack-2014/TRANSCRIPT.md) tips). |
| 23 | ✅ | **Missile trading** — free-form deals online? | Decided (2026-10-03, [RULE-SUGGESTIONS](RULE-SUGGESTIONS.md)): no trading. The print baseline has none, and an engine can't enforce deals. |
