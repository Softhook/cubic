WHAT WE DRAW
What we draw is three maps in one picture: red is height, green marks smooth areas (ice, pad, roofs), blue is brightness (pale tanks, crater splash).
1 · Relief. Noise, warped like the nebula, adds bumps everywhere except the smooth areas.
2 · One light. The sun at 22°, from the upper left, lights ground, craters and buildings alike.
3 · Shadows. The height map, shifted down-sun four times, darkens whatever sits behind something taller.
4 · Finish. Brightness, a cool-to-warm grey scale, a glint on ice and fine grain.
5 · Paint and lights are the only things drawn on top.

BROWSERS DISAGREE
The lighting filter measures slopes per pixel, and each browser sizes it differently. Firefox and Safari agree once the slope scale is pinned; Chrome ignores that and draws much rougher ground.
So that every player sees the same moon, draw the ground once in one browser (the print pipeline's Chrome) and ship it as images, or draw it with our own code on a canvas.
