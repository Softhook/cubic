ONE TILE, THREE STYLES

The same refinery tile and terrain, drawn three ways from the same data
A · SURVEY
Shaded relief from noise, lit from one side. The richest look, and every tile can be seeded differently. Drawn once to images, as tiles are today.
B · LUNAR CHART
Inspired by NASA's 1960s airbrushed lunar charts: paper, contours and hachures. Prints cleanly and still reads in black and white.
C · OPS DISPLAY
Glowing lines on dark, nothing else. The lightest to draw live; suits phones and a tactical-HUD feel.
