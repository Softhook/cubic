FIELD BULLETIN 03 · OUTLINES

MECH SILHOUETTES

The lineup is to one scale; each panel keeps a person (1.8 m) beside the mech. Heavy and low at 1, light and spindly at 6.
1 BREACHER
Close combat. Hunched, shoulders riding above a sunk head, heavy armoured forearms, and twin breaching spikes on one fist. Wide braced stance.
2 BEHEMOTH
Battle carrier, the biggest outline. A flat deck high on long legs, crane arms hanging to the ground with clamps, a container in the cradle on top and a tow ring beneath.
3 TACTICIAN
Swaps places with a friend. A domed command hull on crouched legs, long arms, a comms mast and a dish. The beacon below the dome marks where the other mech arrives.
4 CHAMELEON
The standard frame: upright and boxy on straight legs. Every limb is a hardpoint, here a missile box and a long gun, one swap from a Tactician or a Sniper.
5 SNIPER
Long-range gunner. A lean armoured trooper: T-visor helmet, angular pauldrons, a long scoped rifle held across the body at the ready, longer than the mech is wide.
6 WISP
Recon drone. A sensor dome on four tall spindly legs, about twice a person's height. Cheap, light, nothing worth armouring.
Drawn for Regolith after the reference sheets on the Roster: hunched hulls, small sunk heads, jointed limbs with gaps between them, splayed feet, a person beside every mech. Redrawn, not traced, so no outline is lifted from the stock art.
