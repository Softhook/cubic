HOW REAL CAN CODE GET?
This is near the ceiling for drawn shapes. It reads as a lit, worn machine, but as an illustration, not a photo. What carries it: one light for everything, lit tops and dark undersides on big plates, shadows where parts overlap, wear on the edges, a warm rim from Jupiter.
What it can't do is perspective, curved metal and how materials catch light. That needs a 3D model: build each mech once, render it in the same light from a few angles, and use the renders for card art, dice faces and the board.
At print size most of the rivets vanish. A card needs a strong silhouette and clear light more than fine detail.
