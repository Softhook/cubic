MECH DICE

The six ship classes become six mechs seen from above. The pips are part of each drawing, as on Cubic's ship faces.
1 BASTION
Dug-in turret walker. Was the Battlestation
2 HERALD
Command carrier with a cargo cradle. Was the Flagship
3 BREACHER
Assault frame, two heavy arms. Was the Destroyer
4 WARDEN
Line mech, long gun and missile pod. Was the Frigate
5 KESTREL
Jump-jet. Takes the hex diagonal. Was the Interceptor
6 WISP
Recon drone, fast and fragile. Was the Scout
TWO SIDES
Compact cyan and Union oxide stand in for the four player colours. The line drawing stays white on every colour.
SUPPRESSED (IDEA)
A striped token or sleeve for a pinned mech, like Undaunted's striped counter backs. Only if a rule needs it.
SIDE VIEW, FOR CARD ART
One built-up silhouette per class, scaled by how heavy it is. The same parts can be posed for the card scenes.
