FIELD BULLETIN 01 · THE WORLD

REGOLITH

A war for the ice of Mars
Regolith: the loose dust and broken rock that covers the moon and Mars. Everything here is built on it, and everything gets buried in it.
I · THE SHIPS STOPPED

Generations ago China, America, Europe and the South each founded settlements on Mars, to send water and power back to Earth. Then contact with Earth was lost and the ships stopped coming. Nobody on Mars knows why, and nobody alive remembers Earth.

II · THE ICE

What was left: four settlements that had never trusted each other, a few refineries and reactors, and the ice. Ice is drinking water, air and rocket fuel. There was never enough near the surface for everyone, and now no one will send more.

III · THE FRAMES

The mechs were mining and construction walkers: they dug ice, hauled tanks, raised domes. The same chassis takes a drill, a crane, a sensor mast or a cannon. In the first winter of the shortage every workshop on Mars learned to fit cannons.

CLAIM IT.

Nobody can afford to destroy what they are fighting for: a cratered refinery feeds no one. So the war is fought for control. Walk your mechs into the ground around a site, hold it, and plant your claim beacons where the others can't pull them out. On the moon it is the same war, earlier and colder, in the dark polar craters.

THE FOUR SIDES

Spin-offs of the Earth powers that founded them, long ago. They kept the old names and flags, and answer to nobody on Earth.

SEAT 1 · BLUE
Yuropa Commune

Ice miners from the north polar cap, the oldest settlement. Run by their work crews.

Wants: to keep the ice that was theirs first.

SEAT 2 · RED
Zhong Combine

What is left of the Earth-chartered company: the contracts, the refineries and the best workshops.

Wants: to keep running Mars as a business.

SEAT 3 · AMBER
Merika Free Port

Traders and fixers from the volcanic highlands, who own the reactors and owe nobody.

Wants: power, and the price of it.

SEAT 4 · GREEN
Austral Collective

Farm settlements in the deep Hellas basin: the most people and the thirstiest crops.

Wants: water, before the next harvest fails.

WHAT IT FEELS LIKE

Scarcity
Water is the prize behind every objective. Nobody fights for glory.
Make do
Refitted, patched, salvaged, never new. A wrecked mech comes back from the salvage yard as something else.
Weight
Mechs are slow and plant themselves. Ground is held by standing on it, not by racing across it.
Ground
Dust, craters, ice, wind and sinkholes. The map is a place, not a grid in space.
Working people
Crews, foremen and engineers. No chosen ones, no aliens, no superweapons.
Printed matter
The settlements' own posters, work orders and field manuals: flat inks, bold shapes, stencilled numbers.
