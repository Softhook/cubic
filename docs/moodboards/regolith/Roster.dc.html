FIELD BULLETIN 02 · THE FRAMES

MECH ROSTER

One chassis, six fits. A mech's die is its current fit: lower is heavier and harder to beat, higher is lighter and quicker. A refit re-rolls the die.
DIE 1 · BATTLESTATION
{{m1}}
Dug-in turret walker
Free Attack. Attack an adjacent enemy without using its move.
Sits on the ground with its gun already trained. Squat, wide stance, braced legs, turret on top.
DIE 2 · FLAGSHIP
{{m2}}
Command carrier
Carry & Move. Carry a nearby mech as part of its move, then drop it next to it.
Hauls other frames in its cargo cradle and leads the column. Flat wide body slung between long crane arms.
DIE 3 · DESTROYER
{{m3}}
Tunnelling assault frame
Swap. Swap places with any of your other mechs.
Bores through the ice tunnels and surfaces where another mech stood. Hunched, heavy arms, drill and ram.
DIE 4 · FRIGATE
{{m4}}
Line mech, modular hardpoint
Change to 3 or 5. Refit as a {{m3}} or a {{m5}}.
The standard frame, one arm swap away from either. Upright and boxy, long gun, missile pod.
DIE 5 · INTERCEPTOR
{{m5}}
Jump-jet gunship
Shoot. Attack the first enemy in a clear straight line, without moving.
Fires from the top of its jump, down a line of hexes. Top-heavy gun body on thin legs, jets on the back.
DIE 6 · SCOUT
{{m6}}
Recon drone
Free Refit. Re-roll this mech for free.
Cheap and fragile, swapped out without a second thought. Small pod on spindly legs, or lifted off the ground.

SILHOUETTE REFERENCE

Twelve front views, read in rows and columns from the top left. The six mechs should tell apart by outline alone, in one flat ink, as these do.

Heavy and low at 1, light and spindly at 6. A habitat dome is always bigger than a mech.
