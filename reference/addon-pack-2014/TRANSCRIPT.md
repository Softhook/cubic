# Quantum Add-on Pack (FunForge, 2014): French edition

Transcription of `cards-1.webp` and `cards-2.webp`, scans of the French edition of the official
add-on pack (*Quantum Surcharge Add-on Pack*, © 2014 FunForge). "Surcharge quantum" is the French
name for **Quantum Entanglement**. Each card is given in French with an English translation. French
card names map to: Cérébral = Cerebral, Tyrannique = Tyrannical, Réorganisation = Reorganization,
Furtif = Stealthy, Astucieux = Clever, Obstiné = Stubborn, Flexible = Flexible, Curieux = Curious,
Tactique = Tactical, Stratégique = Strategic, Vorace = Ravenous, Juste = Righteous,
Dangereux = Dangerous, Pilleur = Plundering, Belliqueux = Warlike, Féroce = Ferocious,
Agression = Aggression, Infâmie = Infamy.

## Rules: Quantum Entanglement (*Règles*, 2 cards)

> La surcharge quantum est un ensemble de règles alternatives qui étend les possibilités tactiques
> de fin de jeu et de construction de cartes sectorielles. Vous ne pouvez normalement avoir qu'UN
> cube quantum sur une planète. Cependant, si le SEUL espace disponible sur toute la carte se
> trouve sur une planète sur laquelle vous avez déjà un cube, alors vous POUVEZ placer plusieurs
> cubes sur cette même planète.
>
> Le cas particulier où vous placez un second (troisième ou quatrième) cube de la même couleur sur
> une planète est appelé surcharge quantum. Lorsque vous construisez avec la surcharge quantum,
> ajoutez 3 à la valeur de la planète pour chaque cube que vous y avez déjà placé. N'oubliez pas :
> la surcharge quantum n'est possible que si vous n'avez pas d'autre possibilité de placer un cube
> quantum. Exemple : vers la fin du jeu, la seule place où vous pouvez construire un cube quantum
> est une planète de taille 8 sur laquelle vous avez déjà un cube quantum. La surcharge quantum va
> augmenter la valeur de la planète à 11 (8+3) pour les besoins de la construction d'un second cube
> à cet endroit. Si vous atteignez l'Infâmie, vous pouvez placer un cube à cet endroit.

Quantum Entanglement is an alternative set of rules that widens endgame tactics and map design.
Normally you may have only ONE quantum cube on a planet. However, if the ONLY space available on the
whole map is on a planet where you already have a cube, you MAY place several cubes on that planet.
Placing a second (third or fourth) cube of the same colour on a planet is called quantum
entanglement. When you construct with entanglement, add 3 to the planet's value for each cube you
already have there. Remember: entanglement is only possible if you have no other way to place a
cube. Example: near the end of the game, the only place you can construct is a size-8 planet where
you already have a cube; entanglement raises its value to 11 (8+3) for constructing a second cube
there. **If you reach Infamy, you may place a cube there.**

## Expert FAQ (*F.A.Q. niveau expert*)

- **Can a ship move on the turn it is deployed?** Yes. Ships can move/attack on the turn they are
  deployed.
- **If a ship goes to the scrapyard, can it move a second time in the same turn?** No. A ship can
  move (or move/attack) only once per turn. Each ship can also use its special ability only once
  per turn. This is still true if the ship is destroyed.

## Expert FAQ 2 (*F.A.Q. expert 2*)

- **What if my dominance reaches 6 during my advance-card phase?** It can happen, e.g. if you
  choose the tactic card Aggression at dominance 4 or 5. You reach Infamy: place a quantum cube,
  reset your dominance to 1 and choose a new advance card.
- **What if my dominance reaches 6 during another player's turn?** This can also happen if you have
  Stubborn. Place a quantum cube immediately, reset your dominance to 1, and choose your card at the
  end of the active player's advance-card phase.

## FAQ (untitled cards)

- **Must the Flagship move to use its Transport ability?** Yes, it must move 1 or 2 spaces.
- **Can I transport and attack at the same time?** No. The Flagship can't attack while it
  transports. The transported ship must be dropped on an empty space. It may move/attack normally
  afterwards, as an action separate from the transport.
- **Can I take other actions during my advance-card phase?** No. You can't take actions or use your
  ships' special abilities during your advance-card phase.
- **Can I keep tactic cards for a later turn?** No. Tactic cards are always played immediately, as
  soon as you choose them.
- **Do die values wrap around?** No; e.g. Flexible can't change a 1 into a 6 or vice versa.
- **If an attacking ship survives, where does it go?** The attacker survives and moves back to the
  square from which it attacked (not back to where its move started).
- **Isn't Curious a bit too strong?** For some, yes; the pack includes an alternative Curious card
  for those who want a weaker version.

## Command card tips (*Astuces : cartes de commandement*)

- With **Tactical**, you can only move YOUR ships.
- With **Strategic**, your attacking ship gets the bonus only if it attacks from a square next to
  one of your other ships.
- With **Ravenous** and **Righteous**, you get both benefits, so your dominance is not reduced when
  one of your ships is destroyed.
- If your opponent uses **Dangerous** on you and all your ships are destroyed, you can't benefit
  from **Plundering** or **Warlike**.
- Your total combat value can drop below 1 with cards like **Ferocious** and **Strategic**.
- You can't use **Cerebral** at dominance 1, nor **Tyrannical** at research 1.
- To use **Reorganization**, choose the dice you want to re-roll and **roll them all at the same
  time**. Then place them in orbital positions or in the scrapyard.
- If you use **Reorganization** or **Expansion** and have **Stealthy**, you may place your dice
  anywhere, as long as it isn't next to another die.
- If you use **Reorganization** or **Expansion** and have **Clever**, choose the dice values.
- If you are attacked and have **Stubborn**, you destroy your opponent and gain dominance if your
  combat total is higher than or equal to your opponent's.

## New maps (*Nouvelles cartes*, *Plateaux pour 2/3/4 joueurs*)

> Try these new maps! They were designed for shorter, more intense games and for use with the
> Quantum Entanglement rules. With entanglement you can make much smaller maps, so you can ignore
> the "avoid exclusion" section of the map-design advice. You still need enough cube spaces for
> someone to win! The minimum number of cube spaces is the total number of quantum cubes in play,
> plus one, minus the number of players.

| Players | Map | Cubes per player |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Tactiques (Tactics), Blocus | 4 |
| 3 | Envahisseurs (Invaders), Circumnavigation | 4 |
| 3 | Secteur Bêta (Beta Sector), *basic map for 3 players* | 5 |
| 4 | Eden, Limite Théorique (Theoric Limit) | 4 |
| 4 | Nexus, *advanced map for 4 players* | 6 |

All of them are in [`../bga/maps.json`](../bga/maps.json).
